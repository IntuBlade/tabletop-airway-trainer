# Tabletop airway trainer

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

A medic practicing on an ordinary table with a phone and an IntuBlade. A recorded exercise also lets a reviewer try the workflow without hardware.

## Product Purpose

Place a virtual mannequin on the table, guide the real blade into its virtual mouth, then practice deliberately losing and recovering a simulated airway view. Keep teaching questions and answers tied to the frames the learner marked.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The standalone SwiftUI app uses the phone camera to place the mannequin. Live practice will use the blade camera to track relative to a physical reference registered to the virtual head. The preview and tracking checks have separate evidence. Practice data must stay local unless the learner explicitly requests cloud coaching.
